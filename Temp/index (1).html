Our Story

A Sanctuary for the
Sacred Art of Dance

Lakshita Natyalaya was founded on a single conviction: that Bharatanatyam is not merely a dance form, but a living devotion passed hand to hand, guru to shishya, for over two thousand years. Within our walls, every adavu is taught with the same reverence it was given in the temple courtyards of Tamil Nadu.

Our mission is to nurture disciplined, expressive artists who carry this heritage forward — not as a museum piece, but as a breathing tradition. Our vision looks toward a generation of dancers who understand that grace is built on rigour, and that every gesture carries centuries of meaning.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

At what age can my child begin Bharatanatyam?

We welcome students from age five. Our beginner curriculum is designed around a child's natural development, starting with rhythm and posture before formal adavus.

Do you offer online classes for students abroad?

Yes. We run structured live online batches alongside our in-studio classes, with the same syllabus and periodic in-person intensives where possible.

How long does it take to prepare for an Arangetram?

Most students train for eight to twelve years before their debut recital, though the timeline depends on dedication, attendance and individual progress.

What should a beginner bring to their first class?

Comfortable cotton clothing and bare feet are all that's needed to begin. Practice anklets (salangai) are introduced once foundational adavus are secure.

Are performance opportunities included in the fees?

Annual day and academy showcases are included. External competitions, festivals and Arangetram costs are communicated in advance and kept as affordable as possible.
